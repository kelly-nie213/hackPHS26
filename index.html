    How is this calculated?

    For every sunny hour of the weather year, we cast this building's shadow and measure how much public ground (streets, sidewalks, plazas, parks) it covers. Shade on rooftops doesn't count.

    Avg. shade cast is that area averaged over all sunny hours. Shade per home divides it by the homes on the lot (PLUTO residential units). Lower is better.

    Grades compare against every residential building mapped: A = lowest 20% of shade per home, F = highest 20%.

    Simplifications: shade that overlaps a neighbor's shadow still counts; shadows leaving the mapped area aren't counted.
